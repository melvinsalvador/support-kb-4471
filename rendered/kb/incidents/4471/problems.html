The queued parser fix closes only the first ticket. The form hint and the one-day move stay broken after it ships.

The three problems

Parser (#1). The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. “Booked for 7 April” comes back as 4 July. It reaches us through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

Form hint (#2). booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and its tenants read the field day first. People type what the hint tells them, so the date flips. These reports come only through the form, only from the three tenants on booking-v3. A parser fix does not touch them.

One-day move (#3). Not a flip: the date lands a day early, or differs by where the person is. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That is a time-zone conversion. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). They stay filed and counted under the parser, by tenant, the way the cause table counts them, and #3 tracks them as their own bug.

Why one ticket hid three

  • We merged on subject. Decision 0001 merges reports whose subjects match. “Wrong day on the invite” fits all three problems, so all three landed in one ticket.
  • Intake guesses from the browser. The intake tag comes from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant, so it misfiles form-hint reports as parser ones and the other way round. The table above asks the tenant directory instead.
  • The export repeats rows. The ticket system exports in overlapping pages. Counts here are by unique report id.
